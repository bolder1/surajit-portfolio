# Folio — Surajit Dutta's portfolio

## Register
brand — design IS the product. The portfolio's job is to convince a hiring
manager or design-conscious founder in 60 seconds (Quick), 5 minutes (Deep), or
12 minutes (Read) that Surajit can ship enterprise software that doesn't feel
like enterprise software.

## Users
- **Hiring managers at design-led product companies.** Skim three portfolios
  before lunch, decide who's worth a call. They want signal — the work, the
  thinking, the aesthetic — fast.
- **Senior designers / DRIs evaluating peers.** Will pattern-match craft within
  ten seconds. Will close the tab if it looks like AI-default SaaS-cream.
- **Founders looking for an enterprise-product designer.** Mostly checking that
  Surajit understands real workflows (IAM, IT, security teams) and can deliver
  a system, not just screens.

## Product purpose
A portfolio in the form of a printed newspaper — the *Folio*. Three
experiences served from a first-visit chooser:
1. **Read** — full magazine on `/`, drop caps + pull-quotes + the 8-step
   process spread.
2. **Visualize · Quick** (`/visualize/quick`) — six animated scenes, ≈ 60s.
3. **Visualize · Deep** (`/visualize/deep`) — twelve animated scenes, ≈ 5 min,
   illustrated process, portrait, work-stack, journey, principles.
Plus utility pages: `/work` archive, `/work/[slug]` case studies, `/about`.

## Brand voice
Editorial, restrained, deliberately *not* "designer-portfolio-cream." Print
magazine register — datelines, folios, section tags, italic accents,
hanging punctuation, drop caps, quiet authority. First-person, plain prose.

## Anti-references
- ❌ Awwwards-default 3D scrolling portfolio with gradient blobs.
- ❌ SaaS-cream "minimal" portfolio with one product image and two stats.
- ❌ Cyber-glow / neon-on-black tech portfolio.
- ❌ Y2K-revival, brutalist-Notion, Vercel-default geist + zinc.
- ❌ Generic icon-grids. The "domains in a 2×2" pattern only works because each
  cell is bespoke.

## Strategic principles (written by Surajit, used as voice anchors)
1. **Make complex feel calm.** Right thing on screen, leave everything else
   off. Enterprise UI fails when it asks the user to hold the system in head.
2. **Systems then surfaces.** Tokens, components, patterns first. Screens get
   cheap; the design effort moves to workflow logic.
3. **Ship then sharpen.** First version is for learning. Get a flow into
   reviewers' hands and iterate.

## Constraints that bind every page
- Cream paper `#f3ecde`, deep ink `#161109`, one vermilion accent `#d8331a`.
- Two faces only: Instrument Serif (display + italic) + Geist Mono (eyebrows,
  meta, registration marks).
- No em-dashes in copy.
- Line-work illustrations only (no fills except the accent).
- Must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Must SSR cleanly — round any trig-derived coordinates so server and client
  emit identical strings.
