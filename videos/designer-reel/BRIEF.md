## Intent

This is a portfolio intro reel about Surajit **as a designer**: who he is, his path into design, what he does, how he works, what he's good at, and proof in real numbers. It isn't about security products or one employer. Enterprise work is one chapter, not the whole story. It should feel personal, crafted and cinematic, and it must not look AI-generic. It has full sound design and music. He'll show it on his own portfolio site, so there are no social formats.

## Theme (from Surajit, overrides earlier look notes)

- **Retro style, cyberpunk colours, Apple-style liquid glass on the elements.** The overall vibe is energetic, and the experience must feel like one cohesive piece. This is his own take, and it replaces the oxblood site palette for this video.
- **No AI in the video:**
  - Nothing generated by AI: no AI images, video, voice or music. Everything is hand-built motion design, synthesised sound, and his real screenshots and photo.
  - No AI talk on screen: no "AI-native", "orchestrate with AI", Figma Make or Claude lines, and no AI-workflow framing. The 3 weeks → 5 days result stays as a design-speed fact.

## Customizations

- One continuous neon light trail is the visual through-line. It starts as a circuit trace from his engineering years, becomes user flows, then his design system's single stroke weight, and finally signs off under his name.
- Synthesised retrowave / synth-pop music at 120 BPM (energetic: driving arps, gated snares), with every cut on the beat (1 beat = 15 f).
- Count-ups on the numbers (Doto dot-matrix).
- **Full SFX coverage, intuitive and musical** (user request): every visible action gets the sound you would expect, and the same action always gets the same sound. Tonal SFX are pitched to the track's key. The full action→sound map is in `SOUND.md`.
